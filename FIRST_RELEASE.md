# Introducing Sigflo

Today we're open sourcing Sigflo.

Sigflo began as a personal project.

A simple question drove its creation:

**Why is learning trading so difficult?**

Most platforms assume users already understand markets.

Most educational resources focus on theory.

Many communities focus on predictions rather than understanding.

We wanted something different.

A place where people could learn through practice.

A place to experiment safely.

A place to understand the language of trading.

Over time, Sigflo evolved into a trading workspace featuring paper trading, exchange integrations, analytics, automation, and tools designed to help users grow their skills.

Now we're making that work public.

## Why Open Source?

Trust matters.

Financial software should be transparent.

Open source allows anyone to inspect the code, contribute improvements, and participate in shaping the future of the project.

We believe better tools are built together.

## What Happens Next?

This is only the beginning.

We are actively developing Sigflo and welcome:

- Contributors
- Testers
- Designers
- Educators
- Traders
- Developers

If our mission resonates with you, we'd love your help.

## Our Goal

We do not aim to create the largest trading platform.

We aim to create the best environment for learning trading.

One trade at a time.

Welcome to Sigflo.
