# Sigflo Roadmap

This roadmap represents our current direction and priorities.

The order may change as we learn from users and contributors.

## Phase 1: Foundation

**Current focus**

- Improve platform stability
- Refine paper trading
- Strengthen exchange integrations
- Improve performance analytics
- Improve onboarding experience
- Expand documentation
- Public open-source launch

## Phase 2: Learning Experience

- Trading glossary
- Interactive concept explanations
- Guided learning paths
- Trade review workflows
- Educational insights
- Learning-focused analytics

**Goal:** Help users understand why trades succeed or fail.

## Phase 3: Experimentation

- Strategy testing tools
- Bot improvements
- Simulation environments
- Risk analysis tools
- Trade replay functionality

**Goal:** Create a safe environment for exploration and learning.

## Phase 4: Intelligence

- AI-assisted explanations
- AI trade reviews
- Pattern discovery
- Market education tools
- Personalized learning recommendations

**Goal:** Use AI to teach, not to replace thinking.

## Long-Term Vision

Sigflo becomes the best place on the internet to learn trading through practice.

Not through promises.

Not through hype.

Through experience.

## Not Currently Prioritized

- Social media feeds
- Influencer integrations
- Signal marketplaces
- Excessive gamification
- Features that encourage reckless trading

We optimize for learning, not engagement metrics.
