Support

Need help with Sigflo? We’ll point you in the right direction.

How we can help

Whether you’ve hit a bug, have a question about your account, or need help understanding how something works, this page is here to help.

We’re still early, so support may be lighter than a mature platform — but we want the experience to feel clear, calm, and responsive.

  • Account access issues
  • Login or authentication problems
  • Broker connection problems
  • Bugs or technical issues
  • Questions about features or platform behavior
  • Billing questions, if applicable
  • General feedback during beta

Contact methods

Email support support@sigflo.group
In-app support form Use the in-app support or feedback form for the fastest context, if available.
Beta feedback If you’re reporting a bug or sharing an improvement idea, label it clearly so it can be triaged properly.

Before you contact support

The clearer the report, the easier it is to investigate.

  • The email address associated with your account
  • The device and browser you were using
  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened instead
  • Screenshots, if relevant
  • Whether the issue is ongoing or intermittent

Important note

Sigflo support can help with product and account issues, but we do not provide financial advice, trade recommendations, personal portfolio guidance, or legal and tax advice.

If your question relates to a trading decision, you should make your own assessment or speak with an appropriate licensed professional.

FAQ

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset option on the login screen, if enabled.

Why is a signal or chart not updating?

During beta, some data or UI elements may occasionally lag or fail to refresh correctly.

Can support tell me whether to take a trade?

No. Sigflo support cannot provide financial advice or trade recommendations.

Can I report bugs?

Yes — bug reports are especially helpful during beta.

Response expectations

We aim to review support requests as promptly as possible, but response times may vary, especially during beta.

Some issues may require additional investigation, and some feature requests may be noted for future improvement rather than resolved immediately.
