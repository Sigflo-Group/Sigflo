Privacy Policy — Sigflo

Last updated: 14/04/2026

Overview

Sigflo is a trading interface that provides AI-assisted signals, market analysis, and execution tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Email address and account details
  • Usage data and interactions within the app
  • Device and technical information (IP, browser, device type)

Trading & Financial Data

If you connect Sigflo to third-party services (such as cryptocurrency exchanges):

  • We may access account data such as balances, positions, and market activity
  • Access is limited to permissions granted via API keys
  • We do not store withdrawal permissions

How We Use Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide trading insights and signals
  • Improve platform performance and features
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Communicate updates and product information

Financial Disclaimer

Sigflo does not provide financial advice. All signals, insights, and tools are for informational purposes only. Users are responsible for their own trading decisions and risk management.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics)
  • Authentication providers
  • Third-party APIs (e.g. exchange integrations)

Security

We implement security measures including encrypted connections and restricted access controls. However, no system is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
