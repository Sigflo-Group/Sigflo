We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, improve, and support the platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect several categories of information depending on how you use Sigflo.

  • Account information: such as your name, username, email address, login details, and profile information.
  • Technical information: such as device type, browser type, IP address, operating system, app version, and log data.
  • Usage information: such as pages visited, features used, interactions with signals or alerts, and session activity.
  • Support information: such as messages, screenshots, attachments, and issue reports you submit to support.
  • Transaction or subscription information: such as plan status, billing metadata, and payment-related records from payment providers, where applicable.
  • Connected account data: if you connect a broker, exchange, or other third-party service, we may receive limited account or integration-related data needed to provide the service.

2. How We Use Information

We may use your information to operate and improve Sigflo, including to:

  • create and manage your account
  • authenticate users and secure access
  • deliver platform features, alerts, analytics, and support
  • maintain performance, reliability, and platform security
  • investigate bugs, abuse, fraud, or misuse
  • understand product usage and improve the user experience
  • communicate with you about support, account activity, product changes, or legal updates
  • process subscriptions, billing events, or operational notifications where relevant

3. Broker, Exchange, and Integration Data

If you connect Sigflo to a broker, exchange, or another third-party platform, we may process certain integration data necessary to enable connected features.

This may include things like:

  • account identifiers
  • connection status
  • portfolio or balance information
  • open positions or order-related metadata
  • API-related status information

We only intend to use such data as needed to support the product experience, integrations, and related functionality.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information in the ordinary sense of selling user data for unrelated third-party marketing.

We may share information in limited circumstances, such as:

  • with service providers who help us operate the platform, such as hosting, analytics, customer support, authentication, or payment providers
  • with connected third-party services when you choose to link them to your account
  • where required by law, regulation, legal process, or lawful government request
  • to enforce our terms, protect rights, investigate misuse, or address fraud and security issues
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable legal requirements

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain operational records.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, the nature of your relationship with Sigflo, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Transfers

Depending on how Sigflo is operated and hosted, your information may be processed or stored in jurisdictions outside your own.

By using the platform, you understand that data may be transferred to and processed in locations where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, such as the right to:

  • request access to personal information we hold about you
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • request deletion of certain information
  • object to or restrict certain processing in some circumstances
  • request a copy of certain information in a portable form where applicable
  • opt out of certain non-essential communications

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the laws that apply to you.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Sigflo may use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies to keep the platform functioning, remember preferences, understand usage patterns, improve performance, and support security.

Some of these technologies may be necessary for the platform to work properly, while others may help with measurement and improvement.

10. Children’s Privacy

Sigflo is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by law.

If you believe information has been provided by a child inappropriately, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate steps.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we may revise the “Last updated” date at the top of the page.

Your continued use of Sigflo after an updated policy becomes effective may constitute acceptance of the revised policy, subject to applicable law.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy-related request, please contact Sigflo through the support page or your designated privacy contact method.

Email: support@sigflo.group

You should review this Privacy Policy alongside Sigflo’s Terms of Use, Risk Disclosure, and Beta Disclaimer.

Privacy matters. We aim to handle information carefully, use it responsibly, and keep the platform as secure and trustworthy as reasonably possible.
